## What's new

- **Pay for Pro by card:** subscribe on Gumroad ($10 per month), then paste the licence key in the Buy Pro window. Pro turns off by itself when the subscription ends. Easypaisa and crypto still work.
- **Gaming clips:** a new Home card for gameplay with little or no talking. It finds the big moments from sound and action, and Gemini checks the video frames to pick the best ones.
- Gaming titles and hooks are now written from the final clip's frames, so they match what you see.
- "Behind the person" captions: if no person is found (for example in games), the big word now sits just below the top of the video instead of going missing.
- Gaming Studio is simpler: face-tracking options are hidden, and a note tells you a Gemini key is needed.
- The update window now shows short notes for this update only.
