## What's new

- **New Buy Pro window (3 steps):** 1) choose a plan (Monthly $10 or Yearly $100, save 17% / $20), 2) choose how to pay (Easypaisa, Crypto, Kelviq or Gumroad), 3) pay. Easypaisa and Crypto show the amount for the chosen plan, your device ID and Send proof buttons. Kelviq and Gumroad (now with their logos; yearly card checkout works on Kelviq; Gumroad shows "Currently not available" for now) show a pay button and a box to paste your licence key.
- **Paste a Video Link:** now also downloads Twitch past broadcasts and clips (and many other video sites), not only YouTube. Live streams that are still running are refused with a clear message (live import is planned for later).
- **Forest background:** a soft misty-forest picture now sits behind Projects, Settings and Studio.
- **Kelviq payments (second card option):** Pro keys from Kelviq now work next to Gumroad keys. Paste either key in "I have a key". Pro lasts for the paid month and stops (after 3 days of grace) if the subscription is not renewed. One key works on one computer only.
- **Sideways phone videos fixed:** clips filmed on a phone (stored with a rotate note) were squashed vertically in Already Cut Clips. Their real size is now read correctly, so they are no longer squeezed.
- **AI prompt is now the boss:** besides choosing the moments, your prompt also guides the title, hook and description the AI writes (hook type, tone, what to include).
- **Bigger prompt box:** the prompt can be up to 4000 characters (it was cut at 600), the box is taller and can be resized, with a counter.
- **Pause & continue fix:** a paused project that is continued after closing and reopening the app now keeps the settings it was started with (it no longer falls back to the defaults or overwrites the saved settings file).
- **Progress screen fix:** step statuses (Reading metadata, Extracting audio) and the Terminal Logs now always fill in, even if a live update is missed. The screen also re-checks the engine every moment.
- **New Home page:** forest banner, glossy feature icons, a sidebar with a misty forest, a drag-and-drop box, Recent Projects, Quick Actions, Project Stats and a Go Pro card with a gold crown. AI Boost status now sits at the top right.
- **Projects page:** same new look, with a thumbnail on each project and a New Project button.
- F11 now turns full screen on and off. The drop box text no longer overlaps on smaller windows, and the Podcast Sample button is gone.
- Edit clip: a Hook on/off switch, and a new Layout tab (Fit, Full Crop & Pan, Custom) for each clip.
- Home: the drop box is only for Long and Gaming clips; the other cards keep their own pickers.
- Edit Already Cut Clips: now up to 50 clips at once, each up to 5 minutes.
- The app now adapts to smaller laptop screens: slimmer sidebar, five feature cards in one row, and the side panels move below.
- **Pay for Pro by card:** subscribe on Gumroad ($10 per month), then paste the licence key in the Buy Pro window. Pro turns off by itself when the subscription ends. Easypaisa and crypto still work.
- **Gaming clips:** a new Home card for gameplay with little or no talking. It finds the big moments from sound and action, and Gemini checks the video frames to pick the best ones.
- Gaming titles and hooks are now written from the final clip's frames, so they match what you see.
- "Behind the person" captions: if no person is found (for example in games), the big word now sits just below the top of the video instead of going missing.
- Gaming Studio is simpler: face-tracking options are hidden, and a note tells you a Gemini key is needed.
- The Buy Pro window is cleaner and the crown icon is now a gem.
- The update window now shows short notes for this update only.
